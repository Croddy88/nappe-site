Designed for a cook with wet hands and thirty seconds to spare.

Made on the line.

Nappé Studio was built by a working chef who got tired of chasing the current version of a spec. Every screen starts from the same question: can someone use this during a Friday push?

The name comes from nappe, the point where a sauce coats the back of a spoon. It is the test every cook learns early, and the standard we hold the studio to.
