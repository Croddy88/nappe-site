Volume pricing on Group

The more you run, the less each one costs. Location prices step down like tax brackets: your first 3 locations are $149 each, the next ones less. The concept discount then comes off the location total.

    Questions

    What counts as a person?

    Anyone with their own login. A cook, a manager, a server who uses the Menu Guide. People who leave can be removed any time and stop counting.

    What's the difference between a concept and a location?

    A concept is a brand with its own menu and recipes. A location is a place it operates. One taqueria with three locations is one concept, three locations.

    Can I change plans later?

    Yes, up or down, any month. Your recipes, training records and settings stay exactly where they are.

    Is there a contract?

    Monthly plans can be canceled any time. Yearly plans are paid up front for twelve months, with two of them free.

    Do you help us get set up?

    Every plan comes with a welcome tour and training modules. On Group and Enterprise we load your existing recipes and line builds for you.

    Can you set everything up for us?

    Yes. On Enterprise and custom plans we can build your recipes, line builds, menus, station maps and training for a one-time setup fee. Send us what you have and we’ll quote it with your plan.

    Does it connect to my point of sale?

    Food cost reports work today with sales typed in for each period. We also connect point-of-sale systems so sales come in on their own and dish sales show true food cost item by item. Tell us what you use and we'll quote it.

    Can I try it first?

    Open the demo studio. It signs you straight in with a sample restaurant group and a short guided tour.
