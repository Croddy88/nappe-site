Effective November 15, 2026.

This policy explains what information Nappe Studio LLC ("Nappé Studio," "we") collects through nappestudio.com and the Nappé Studio app (app.nappestudio.com), how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: legal@nappestudio.com.

1. Two kinds of information

  • Information our clients put in the app. Restaurants (our clients) use the app to manage their recipes, training and staff. For that information, the restaurant is in charge and we process it on their behalf. If you work at a restaurant that uses Nappé Studio, contact your employer about your information; we will help them respond.
  • Information about our own visitors and customers. For people who visit our website, ask for a demo, or buy a subscription, we decide how the information is used, as described below.

The app is for restaurant businesses and their staff. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, showing each person what their role allows, saving their work, sending the notifications and emails they asked for.
  • To provide AI features a restaurant turns on, such as answering questions from its own specs, reading invoices, translating and drafting menu text.
  • To bill customers, keep signed agreements and send account notices.
  • To keep the service secure, back it up nightly, find and fix problems, and prevent misuse.
  • To answer demo requests and support questions.
  • To improve the service using combined statistics that do not identify anyone.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use restaurant content to train AI models.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Nappé Studio, under contracts that limit their use of it (the full list is in the Data Processing Addendum): Google (Firebase database, sign-in and file storage, and the Gemini service that turns voice notes into text), Netlify (website and app hosting, and our website forms), Anthropic (AI features), Postmark (email), and Stripe (payments). We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of our business, with notice to customers.

5. Cookies and similar technology

The app stores small amounts of information in your browser (for example, your language, theme, sign-in and last page) so it works the way you left it, and it can keep a copy on your device for use without a connection. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends them. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep a restaurant's information while it has an active subscription, then for 60 days so it can export it or come back, then delete it. Nightly backups are kept for 90 days. Billing records and signed agreements are kept as long as tax and contract law require. Demo requests are kept for 2 years unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

7. Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers. Each person signs in with their own account, and the database itself checks every request against that person's role and restaurant, so staff only see what their role allows and one restaurant can never see another's data. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify the affected restaurants and people as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Staff can update their name and turn notifications on or off in the app.
  • Restaurants can export or delete their data at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information. Email legal@nappestudio.com. If the information belongs to a restaurant client, we will pass your request to them and help them respond.

9. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date at the top. For material changes we will also email customers.
